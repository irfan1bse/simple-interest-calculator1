# Simple Interest Calculator

A simple Bash calculator that calculates simple interest.

## Formula

Simple Interest = (Principal × Rate × Time) / 100

Where:

- Principal is the initial amount of money
- Rate is the interest rate per year
- Time is the period in years

## Example

If the principal is 1000, the rate is 5%, and the time is 2 years:

Simple Interest = (1000 × 5 × 2) / 100 = 100

## Usage

Run the Bash script and enter the principal, rate, and time when prompted.
